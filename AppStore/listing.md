## Subtitle

Your passwords, only yours.

## Promotional text

A private password manager for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Face ID or Touch ID, passkeys, one-time codes, and an
encrypted vault that syncs only through your own iCloud.

## What's New in 2.0

A rebuilt Asteria Keys. Face ID and Touch ID are reliable after the first unlock. The vault syncs ciphertext
through your private iCloud database, with conflict copies instead of silent loss. Apple Intelligence can draft a
login from a sentence, find items from a description, and explain your security findings without ever seeing a
secret. New Siri can compose a login and open it for review. A new Liquid Glass interface, passkeys, one-time
codes, AutoFill on both platforms, and a full security review.

## Description

YOUR PASSWORDS, ONLY YOURS

Asteria Keys keeps your logins, passkeys, one-time codes, cards, identities, Wi-Fi passwords, and secure notes in
one calm place. The vault is encrypted on your device. Your master password never leaves it.

BUILT FOR FACE ID AND TOUCH ID

Unlock with Face ID on iPhone and iPad, or Touch ID on Mac. The key is kept in the keychain, behind your device's
own authentication. If biometrics are unavailable, your master password and recovery key still work.

SYNC THROUGH YOUR OWN ICLOUD

A change made on your iPhone appears on your Mac, and the other way around. Keys stores only encrypted records in
your private iCloud database. Apple cannot read them, and Asteria has no server, no account, and no analytics.

APPLE INTELLIGENCE, WITHOUT THE SECRETS

Describe a login and let the on-device model fill in the name, site, and username. Ask for "old logins without a
code" and Keys turns it into a search. The security coach reads counts of findings, never a password, username,
site name, or note. On a device that supports it, and with your permission, the coach can use Apple's Private
Cloud Compute for deeper reasoning.

NEW SIRI

"Compose a login in Asteria Keys." "Find in Asteria Keys." "Lock Asteria Keys." Siri can prepare a login, open
an item, search titles and sites, and check how many items need review. It never speaks a password.

AUTOFILL AND PASSKEYS

Keys fills passwords and one-time codes in other apps on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, after you unlock. Passkeys are
stored in the same encrypted vault.

SECURITY YOU CAN SEE

See reused, short, and old passwords, logins without a one-time code, and a clear score. Fix them one at a time.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No Asteria account. No Asteria server. No analytics. No advertising. The master password never leaves your
devices, and the vault key never leaves the keychain.

Asteria Keys is made by Asteria Technology for iOS 27 and macOS 27.

## App Privacy

**Data Not Collected.** The vault is stored in the person's private iCloud database and is encrypted before it
leaves the device. Asteria Technology cannot read it. There is no analytics, advertising, or third-party SDK.
Apple Intelligence runs on the device; Private Cloud Compute, when enabled, is Apple's service and receives
counts only.
